Siraj AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Siraj AI ("the App") provides AI-powered features and user account services. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account information, such as your email address, display name, and Firebase user identifier.
  • Questions, prompts, and other content you submit to AI features.
  • Usage and technical information needed for request limits, security, troubleshooting, and service reliability.
  • Subscription status and purchase verification data needed to provide paid features. Google Play handles payment processing.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or sensitive information in AI prompts.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create accounts and authenticate users.
  • To provide AI responses and app features.
  • To enforce usage limits and manage subscriptions.
  • To protect the service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • To diagnose errors, improve reliability, and respond to support requests.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. AI Processing

When you submit a question or prompt, relevant content may be sent to our backend service and an AI service provider to generate a response. Processing by third-party providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We may use Google Firebase for authentication and app infrastructure, Google Play for billing, a backend hosting provider to operate the service, and an AI provider to generate responses. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete information already stored by service providers.

For privacy requests, contact the developer through the support contact provided in the Siraj AI Google Play Store listing.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children who are not permitted to use it under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable legal requirements.

8. International Processing

Service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own, subject to applicable laws and their privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support contact provided in the Siraj AI Google Play Store listing.
