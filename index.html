Siraj AI Privacy Policy Last updated: October 26, 2026 Siraj AI ("the App") provides AI-powered features and user account services. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the App. 1. Information We Collect Depending on how you use the App, we may process: - Account information: email address, display name, and Firebase user identifier when you register or sign in. - User content: questions, prompts, and other information you choose to submit to AI features. - Usage and technical information: feature usage, request counts, error logs, and basic technical information needed to maintain security and service reliability. - Subscription information: subscription status and purchase verification data needed to provide paid features. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information in AI prompts. 2. How We Use Information We use information to: - Create and manage accounts and authenticate users. - Provide AI-powered responses and app features. - Enforce free-use limits and manage premium subscriptions. - Protect the App against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access. - Diagnose errors, maintain security, and improve service reliability. - Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations. 3. AI Processing When you submit a question or prompt to an AI feature, the relevant content may be sent to our backend service and an AI service provider to generate a response. Do not submit information you do not want processed in this way. Processing by third-party providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. 4. Service Providers and Sharing We may use service providers to operate the App, including: - Google Firebase for authentication and related app infrastructure. - Google Play for subscription purchases and billing. - Our backend hosting provider to process requests and operate the service. - An AI service provider to generate AI responses. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of users or the service. 5. Data Retention and Security We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and operational requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. 6. Your Choices and Rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may stop using the App at any time. For privacy requests, contact us using the email address provided in the Google Play Store listing. Deleting the App does not necessarily delete information already stored by service providers. Contact us to request deletion where applicable. 7. Children's Privacy The App is not intended for children who are not permitted to use it under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements. 8. International Processing Service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own, subject to applicable laws and their privacy practices. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated revision date. 10. Contact For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the support contact provided on the Siraj AI Google Play Store listing.
